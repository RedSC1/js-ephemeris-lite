# Third-Party Notices

The bundled TSC1 v1 catalog is derived from Gaia DR3, ESA Hipparcos, the Yale
Bright Star Catalogue/BSC5, and project-maintained traditional-star identity
records. TSC1 preserves per-record source identifiers and astrometric source
flags. The catalog contains source astrometry plus a local searchable identity
and alias layer; the TSC1 container and loading code are project work.

The lite selection retains catalog records with visual magnitude `V <= 5.0`,
two project-maintained special-direction records, and the explicit minimum
coverage for the 28 Chinese mansion determinative stars and one representative
of each western zodiac constellation. Alrescha is retained beyond the ordinary
magnitude cut to preserve Pisces coverage.

Redistribution of a converted catalog does not replace the terms, attribution,
or citation guidance of its upstream data providers. Preserve this notice with
redistributed copies of the package or catalog.

- Gaia Archive: https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/
