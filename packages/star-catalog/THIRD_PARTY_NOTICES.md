# Third-Party Notices

The bundled TSC1 v1 catalog is derived from Gaia DR3, ESA Hipparcos, the Yale
Bright Star Catalogue/BSC5, Stellarium sky-cultures, and project-maintained
special-direction records. TSC1 preserves per-record source identifiers and
astrometric source flags. The catalog contains source astrometry plus a local
searchable identity and alias layer; the TSC1 container and loading code are
project work.

The lite selection retains every available catalog record with visual
magnitude `V <= 5.0`, every HIP star used by the line figures in Stellarium's
Chinese sky culture, every HIP star used by the twelve western zodiac line
figures, and two project-maintained special-direction records. Unambiguous
English and Simplified Chinese traditional names are retained as aliases.

The Stellarium material is taken from
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures at revision
`014fbb5e59233d133c22f9811af96b67d05a95c9` and is provided under CC BY-SA.
The Chinese sky culture was initially contributed by Karrie Berglund of
Digitalis Education Solutions, Inc., based on Hong Kong Space Museum star
maps. Sun Shuwei added more than 200 Chinese asterisms and more than 3,000 star
names, primarily based on Yi Shitong's *Chinese and Western Contrast Star Chart
and Catalogue 1950.0*. The Chinese text was reworked by the Stellarium team;
the western sky culture is by the Stellarium team.

or citation guidance of its upstream data providers. Preserve this notice with
